Tablists

Arrow keys move focus and wrap, Home and End jump, Enter or Space opens the focused tab; only the selected tab is a tab stop. Classes: cu-tabs, --navbar, --lined, --dark.

Hover tabs

Two kinds of hover tabs, and either can sit on the left (default) or --right. Pointing at a label (after 100ms) or focusing it shows its picture. They are not a tablist.

Content tabs

The labels are buttons and the action is the button in the panel. A 55% tint covers the whole picture. Tab moves to a label, then into its panel, then on to the next label. Below 768px the labels hide and the panels stack. Shown on the left with the default black current label.

Link tabs

Each label is the link, and its panel is only a picture, with no tint and no tab stop. A carnelian underline grows in under the current label. With __label--rad, an arrow slides in after it when you point at it. Without it, a small boxed arrow fades in at the right from 960px. On phones each link becomes a black bar laid over its picture. Modal dialog

Close takes focus first, Tab stays inside, Esc or a click on the backdrop closes, and focus returns to the button.

Please confirm

You are about to unsubscribe from all communications.

Update message and toast

Confirming the dialog above shows a toast at the bottom right (CDS .ui-toast). It is a polite live region, so it is announced without taking focus. It closes itself after 4 seconds, but stays while the pointer or focus is on it, and Esc or Dismiss closes it. The inline form (cu-update without --toast) stays until dismissed.
