Sliders

The image slider, the banner slider and the card slider. Slides never move on their own for people who ask for reduced motion, there is always a pause button, and everything is reachable with the keyboard.

Image slider

Autoplays every four seconds, with a pause button (data-autoplay). Using any control, or focusing the slider, stops it for good. Left and Right arrows, Home and End on a control move between slides. Pips show from 768px. The second slide holds a video instead of a picture: it plays only while its slide is showing (never with reduced motion) and has its own Pause button.

Card slider

Plain horizontal scrolling with snap points: swipe, trackpad and keyboard work without script, and tabbing to a card scrolls it into view. Script adds the buttons and a pip per screen. Cards per screen change with width; --max-N sets the widest count.
