Motion

Reveals as an element scrolls into view, and parallax (CDS Motion Design). An element reveals once, when its top reaches 85% of the viewport height.

Kept from CDS: the reveal point, timings and distances, and that nothing is hidden until the script has run (.is-scripted). Added for accessibility: with reduced motion everything shows at once and nothing moves, keyboard focus inside a hidden element reveals it, and printing shows everything.
