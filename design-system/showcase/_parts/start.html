Before you start

You need a Cornell NetID and access from your unit. Requests are usually answered within two working days. See the acceptable use guidance for what you may enter.

What you can do

  • Draft a first version from your notes
  • Tighten or rephrase a paragraph
  • Summarise a long document

How to begin

  1. Sign in with your NetID
  2. Choose a project
  3. Paste or upload your text
