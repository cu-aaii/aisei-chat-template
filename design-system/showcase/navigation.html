Navigation

Every CDS navigation variant: bar tones, alignment, active and hover styles, dropdowns, megamenus, vertical lists and section navigation. Resize below 960px and 768px to see the phone forms.

Tones

Light, dark (default), red and blue. The bar picks up its colours from tokens, so each tone also follows light and dark mode. Hover and focus a parent, or press Down on it, to open its menu.

Dropdown menus

Three levels. The bar folds behind a button below 768px, and submenus become accordions below 960px. Keys: Left/Right between items, Down to open and enter, Up/Down inside, Right for a flyout, Left to go back, Escape to close, Tab to leave.
